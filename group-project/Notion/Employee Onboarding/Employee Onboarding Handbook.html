Policies (FAQ)
What's the PTO policy?
Flexible PTO with a 15-day recommended minimum. Log time off in Gusto and your team calendar.
Can I work remotely?
Yes — we're hybrid. Office days are Tuesday and Thursday for NYC-based staff.
How do expenses work?
Submit receipts in Ramp within 30 days. Pre-approval needed above $500.
What's the learning budget?
$1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences.
Who do I contact for IT issues?
Post in #it or email it@company.com — response within 4 business hours.

Why it made the list

No. 01 — Supra RM

Argonaut builds every frame around a single rider, and the Supra RM on the stand was the clearest argument yet for why that matters. Each tube is wound in-house to its own stiffness target, so the front triangle can be planted and the seat tube forgiving without the compromises a moulded frame forces. On the show floor it simply looked resolved — no wasted material, no generic shapes.

The finish did the rest of the talking: a hand-rubbed burnt-orange over raw weave that shifts from rust to ember as you walk around it, paired with a blacked-out cockpit and King hubs in matching anodise. It is the bike the whole hall kept drifting back to.

“A frame that behaves like it was poured for one person, because it was.” — MADE 2026 selection panel

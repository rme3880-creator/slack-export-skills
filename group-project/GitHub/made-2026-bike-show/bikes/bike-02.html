Sweet Spot All-Road

Steel all-road · Bozeman, Montana

Fillet-brazed 4130 & stainless 9.4 kg / 20.7 lb $6,400 frameset

Why it made the list

No. 02 — Sweet Spot All-Road

Adam Sklar has spent a decade refining one idea — a fast drop-bar bike that still wants to be ridden down a rough forest road — and the Sweet Spot is the distilled version. Low trail up front, generous tyre clearance, mounts everywhere, and fillet work so smooth the joints look moulded.

The build leaned all the way into the show's warm palette: a hand-faded orange-to-cream frame, brown cork tape, canvas frame bag and a brass head badge. Practical, a little romantic, and quietly one of the best-riding bikes on the floor.

“The bike you would actually keep if you could only keep one.” — MADE 2026 show-floor judges

Why it made the list

No. 04 — B-Road

As the hometown builder, Breadwinner had something to prove, and the B-Road delivered a textbook do-everything steel gravel bike: room for 45s with fenders, three bottle mounts, rack and bag bosses, and geometry that stays calm fully loaded.

The show bike wore a deep chocolate-brown fade with orange pinstriping and colour-matched steel fenders — unmistakably a MADE 2026 bike, and a reminder that a well-sorted steel frame still covers more real-world riding than almost anything else.

“No party tricks. Just a bike that will outlast its owner.” — MADE 2026 show-floor judges

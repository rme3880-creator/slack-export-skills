Why it made the list

No. 07 — Baxter YBB

The Baxter is the bike Moots has been quietly perfecting for thirty years: a titanium hardtail with just an inch of pivotless rear give from the YBB shock, enough to take the edge off without adding a linkage.

Shown in raw bead-blasted titanium with laser-etched topographic graphics and orange decals, it was the most understated frame in the room and, for a certain kind of rider, the most desirable. It will still be ridden in 2050.

“The long game, welded in titanium.” — MADE 2026 selection panel

Why it made the list

No. 08 — Randonneur

This is classic constructeur practice brought fully up to date: an integrated front rack brazed as part of the fork, dynamo lighting routed inside the tubes, hand-cut lugs, and a handlebar bag sized to the frame from the first sketch.

The deep chocolate paint with tan panels and gold pinstriping was pure old-world, but the clearances, the wide-range drivetrain and the dead-silent wiring were entirely modern. It won the room's ‘bike I would ride across a country’ vote by a mile.

“A hundred years of touring wisdom with none of the compromises.” — MADE 2026 show-floor judges

Why it made the list

No. 05 — Carrizo

The Carrizo is Stinner's answer to the drop-bar bike that has quietly become a mountain bike. Clearance for 2.1 in rubber, a suspension-corrected front end, and a top-tube profile shaped around a full frame bag.

Finished in a matte tan powdercoat with charcoal accents and a bolt-on top-tube pack, it was the bike most likely to have gravel dust on it by Sunday afternoon. Several visitors tried to buy the demo unit on the spot.

“A permission slip to ride further than you planned.” — MADE 2026 selection panel

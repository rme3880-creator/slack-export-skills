Why it made the list

No. 06 — Sub-6 Road

Rob English has built a reputation on hitting weight targets that steel is not supposed to reach, and the Sub-6 makes the point in its name. Under six kilograms, fully built, with a steel main triangle — achieved through obsessive tube selection and machined parts nobody else bothers to make.

The finish was deliberately restrained: a warm gunmetal grey with a single orange seat-tube band, letting the silhouette and the impossibly thin stays carry the stand. A quiet flex of craft.

“Proof that the ceiling for steel is still higher than people think.” — MADE 2026 show-floor judges

Titanium all-road · Boston, Massachusetts

3Al/2.5V & 6Al/4V titanium 8.2 kg / 18.1 lb $9,600 frameset

Why it made the list

No. 03 — Ti All-Road

Firefly's stand is always a masterclass in titanium finishing, and this year's all-road bike raised it again. Anodised in a smoked bronze-grey that reads almost like patinated steel, with masked graphics and a bead-blasted contrast panel, it rewarded a very close look.

Underneath the finish is serious engineering — printed titanium yokes let the chainstays clear a 35 mm tyre with a road-tight rear end, and the ride reports from demo laps were unanimous: quick, quiet, and unbothered by bad pavement.

“Jewellery you are encouraged to ride hard.” — MADE 2026 selection panel
